Sensitivity Finder

A blind flick test that finds the mouse sensitivity you aim best with, then converts it to your games.

How it works
  1. Every round you flick to targets at a different, hidden sensitivity.
  2. Each round gets a score: accuracy divided by median time to hit.
  3. A golden-section search picks the next sensitivity, closing in on your best one.
  4. A curve fitted through all rounds smooths out lucky and unlucky rounds.
  5. The trainer also records whether your first stop goes past the target or short of it.

Use Chrome or Edge for raw mouse input. Turn off any mouse acceleration and play fullscreen for the cleanest results.
